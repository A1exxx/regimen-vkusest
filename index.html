The idea

Most people quit not because they are lazy.

They quit because they take on a load nobody could repeat next Tuesday. This week is about the opposite: finding the volume you will still be doing in a month, in three, in a year.

01

Measure first, plan second

We do not start with a programme. We start with your numbers: resting heart rate, mobility, three days of your actual eating. A plan without a measurement is somebody else's plan.

02

Five movements, not thirty

Squat, hinge, push, pull, carry. Everything else is a variation. We drill the technique with body weight only: no barbells, no gym membership required.

03

Food you will actually eat

Not chicken breast and broccoli for the next twelve months. We take the café's own menu (borsch, cutlets, dumplings) and work out how it fits your numbers. Spoiler: it fits.

Programme

Five days, ten sessions

Morning practice happens on the ground next to the café, evening breakdowns happen in the dining room. Dinner from the week's menu is included in both ticket types.

  1. 22Tue

    Baseline

    Where you actually are. Body composition, resting heart rate, a mobility screen, and a read-through of your three-day food diary. By the evening everyone has their own sheet of numbers instead of a shared handout.

    07:30 Measurements & mobility screen

    18:00 Reading your numbers + dinner

  2. 23Wed

    Foundation

    Technique for the five base movements, body weight only. We film everyone, then show you where the back gives out and why the knee hurts from how you enter the squat rather than from the squat itself.

    07:30 Practice: squat, hinge, carry

    18:00 Video breakdown + dinner

  3. 24Thu

    Fuel

    Kitchen day. The chef breaks the café menu down by composition, you count the protein in your own lunch and build a plate against your own numbers. Tasting of lighter versions of four regulars.

    07:30 Light practice + breakfast

    18:00 Kitchen masterclass

  4. 25Fri

    Pulse

    Cardio that does not wreck you. Forty minutes of walking and running in zone two along Chalong in the morning, heart-rate monitor on everyone. In the evening: why going to failure every session breaks your progress.

    07:30 Zone two along Chalong

    18:00 Heart-rate zones + dinner

  5. 26Sat

    Recovery

    Sleep, mobility, stretching: the part that decides more than one more workout ever will. A repeat measurement, a comparison against Tuesday, and the long family lunch this whole thing was built around.

    08:00 Mobility & stretching

    13:00 Repeat measurement + lunch

Questions

What people usually ask

I do not train at all. Is it too early for me?

The opposite: that is the main audience. All practice is body weight, every movement has a scaled-down version, and the exercise-therapy physician reviews your measurements before you lift anything.

Can I come for a single day only?

Yes, the single-day ticket covers that. Measurements happen on day one and day five though, so the before-and-after comparison only works with the full pass.

What language is it in?

The main language is Russian; all four coaches also speak English and take questions one-to-one. Handouts and your load sheet come in both languages.

What if it rains?

September on Phuket means rain and we planned for it. In heavy rain the morning session moves under the café terrace roof. Shorter format, but it happens.

Is food included?

Yes. The single-day ticket includes one meal that day; the full pass includes all five. Drinks other than water and tea are charged separately.

How do I pay, and can I get a refund?

Fifty per cent deposit by bank transfer or PromptPay, the rest on day one. Full refund if you cancel before 18 September; after that we move your place to the next run.
